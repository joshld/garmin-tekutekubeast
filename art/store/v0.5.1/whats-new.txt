v0.5.1 beta — A small beast, a bigger world

ORIGINAL SIZE, MORE EXPLORATION
Restored the exact v0.4.0 creature size while expanding its horizontal and vertical roaming. Expressive walking, hopping, pauses, reactions and attention-only needs remain.

FOUR OUTDOOR WORLDS
Explore Forest, Meadow, Lakeside and Garden pixel-art scenery. Let your beast's family choose the theme or select another in Settings.

REAL LOCAL SKIES
Sunrise and sunset use cached or approximate location data, with local offline calculations as a fallback. Enjoy dawn, daylight, dusk, moon and stars. Bedtime remains separate from sunset. No continuous GPS, internet requirement or watch-brightness changes.

CLEARER MEMORY, LIVELIER BOUNCE
Every Follow My Lead move now has a neutral pause, including repeats. Normal Beast Bounce stars arrive faster across the three stages; Easy Mode retains slower timing. Existing paused sessions resume without resetting rewards or progress.

BATTERY-CONSCIOUS POLISH
Fewer unchanged screen redraws and save writes, cached sleep-profile reads and a safe duplicate-step shortcut. Hidden/inactive screens stop their animation timers. Genuine new steps and essential care/sleep updates still reconcile.

Your existing pet, evolution, activity history, inventory and collections are retained. Update the existing installation—do not uninstall first.

Development beta: real-watch location availability, outdoor visibility, button comfort, Glance/timeout behaviour and battery life still need acceptance testing. The moon arc is decorative, not a lunar ephemeris.
