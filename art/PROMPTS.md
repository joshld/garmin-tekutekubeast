# First artwork pass

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the supplied overall game concept as a visual reference. The original PNGs are preserved in `art/source`; they are not loaded by the watch. `scripts/prepare_art.py` performs only deterministic size, alpha and palette conversion for the resources.

The first implementation covers shared Egg, Pip and Nimble. Later family species, expression frames and rare forms are not finished. These common stages do not determine the saved family.

## Pip

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game sprite for a 280x280 Garmin watch virtual-pet game TekuTekuBeast. Image 1 is a visual reference only: match the charm and pixel-art rendering of the small green hatchling in the GROVE FAMILY panel of the moodboard, and the cute pointed-ear pet in its watch mockups; ignore all text and branding in that image. Generate one original shared hatchling named Pip: a very cute tiny woodland beast, compact pear-shaped fluffy green body, oversized expressive dark eyes with simple bright highlights, little cream muzzle and belly, two pointed leaflike ears, a tiny leafy tuft, stubby paws and a small curl tail. Front three-quarter view facing slightly right; full body and all ears/tail/feet visible, sitting alert with a gentle happy expression. Strong recognizable silhouette and warm personality, sophisticated hand-crafted retro RPG pixel art, not geometric placeholder circles. Strict pixel-art appearance built as if on a 64x64 logical pixel grid, crisp stepped contours, one-logical-pixel dark forest green outline, restrained internal detail, 5 solid green/cream shades plus dark outline, no soft shading, no glow, no ground shadow. Make every feature readable at 64 pixels tall. Center this ONE character on a genuinely transparent background with generous transparent padding all around, no floor, no framing, no text, no labels, no icons, no other characters. This is the common hatchling before its family branch, so no antlers, wings, fire, aquatic fins or purple magic. Preserve the reference's appealing lively small-creature proportions.

## Egg

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art mystery egg sprite for TekuTekuBeast, a 280x280 Garmin virtual-pet watch game. Image 1 is a visual reference only for art style and palette: the newly designed cute leaf-eared green Pip beast. Generate ONE matching mystery egg that could hatch into that creature: plump upright egg with an irregular cluster of dark leaf-shaped green spots around its lower half, warm pale cream shell, two light green marks near top and a subtle tiny star glint contained beside the shell. Friendly magical woodland feeling, very simple and readable, carefully hand-crafted retro RPG pixel art with chunky crisp stepped pixels and dark green outline. Design as if on a 64x64 logical grid; use only 5 solid greens/cream shades and an outline. No soft shadow, no gradient, no glow, no blur, no text, no labels, no hatched creature, no nest, no scenery. Full egg visible, centered on genuinely transparent background with generous padding. Keep styling consistent with reference sprite; this is an unhatched shared species, no family emblem.

## Nimble

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art juvenile Nimble sprite for TekuTekuBeast Garmin watch game. Image 1 is the approved Pip hatchling design used solely as a character/style reference. Generate the NEXT juvenile evolution of that same creature: a graceful playful woodland fox-cat beast with longer legs, slightly taller torso, two very large pointed leaf-shaped ears, cream muzzle and chest ruff, confident sparkling eyes, layered leaflike fur along back, a longer curled leafy tail, small nimble paws. Green and warm cream coat with a dark forest-green contour. Entire creature in front three-quarter view slightly facing right, standing alert with one forepaw gently lifted, warm expressive friendly personality. Visibly more mature and agile than the seated hatchling but still adorable. No antlers, no wings, no fire or aquatic or magic motifs because the family is not assigned until adulthood. Match the detailed expressive pixel-art spirit of the original moodboard, NOT circles/rectangles placeholder art. Pixel sprite designed as if on a 64x64 logical grid with clearly stepped contours, only 5 green/cream solid shades plus a dark outline, restrained detail readable at 64 pixels tall, no soft shading, no texture, no glow or ground shadow. ONE character only, genuinely transparent background, centered with generous padding, full ears, tail and feet visible, no crop. No lettering, no labels, no borders, no ground, no extra objects.
